About Know Your PC

Understand Your PC. Master Your Tech.

Technology shouldn't feel complicated. Know Your PC helps beginners understand their computers through practical guides, clear explanations and useful troubleshooting advice.

Our mission

Our mission is to make computer knowledge easier to access and understand. We break down technical topics into manageable steps so readers can make more informed decisions about their computers.

Whether you're using a computer for studying, work, gaming or everyday tasks, we're here to help you understand how it works and how to look after it.

What you'll learn

  • PC components: Learn about RAM, processors, storage and computer upgrades.
  • Troubleshooting: Find practical steps for common performance and Windows problems.
  • Software: Understand useful tools, system settings and everyday computer tasks.
  • Security: Learn safer computing habits and ways to reduce malware risks.

Built for beginners

Know Your PC is designed for people who want straightforward explanations without needing an advanced technical background. We aim to provide useful, accessible information for readers in South Africa and beyond.

Before following instructions that change system settings or delete files, take time to understand the steps and back up important information.

Let's learn together

Have a computer question, a topic suggestion or feedback about one of our guides? We'd love to hear from you.
